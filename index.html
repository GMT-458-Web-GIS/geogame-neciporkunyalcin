How to Play

• The game continues until you lose all 3 lives.
• Each round has a 45-second timer.
• For each round, you see a sports-related hint about a world city.
• Use the map to guess where that city is located.
• Click on the map to select your guess, then press Submit Guess.
• Your score is higher when you are closer and faster.
• If your guess is very far from the true city or the time runs out, you lose one life.
